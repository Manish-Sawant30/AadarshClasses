Extra-Curricular Activities

Celebrating patriotism, culture, creativity, and unity!

Enriching Class Life

At Aadarsh Classes, we believe in nurturing patriotism, culture, and unity through our thoughtfully celebrated events. Our extra-curricular calendar includes national celebrations like Independence Day (15th August) and Republic Day (26th January), which instill a deep sense of pride and respect for the nation among students.

We also organize vibrant festivals such as the Ganpati Mahotsav, promoting tradition and togetherness in a joyful atmosphere. The Annual Gathering is a much-awaited occasion that showcases students' talents in music, dance, drama, and more—encouraging confidence, stage presence, and creative expression.

A special highlight of our calendar is the "Student of the Year" award—an inspiring initiative to recognize students who demonstrate all-round excellence in academics, discipline, leadership, and character.
These events not only enrich students' school life but also foster discipline, unity, and a strong cultural foundation.

Ganesh Mahotsav

Aadarsh Classes celebrates Ganpati Mahotsav with great devotion and unity. The classroom is beautifully decorated with fresh flowers, an effort led by our talented girl students. During the celebration, a group of girls respectfully recite 7–8 Aartis, creating a divine atmosphere. For each Aarti, the chief guests are the families of our 10th standard students. Boys enthusiastically play the tabla and blow the shankh. After the Aarti, fun-filled comedy events bring laughter and joy to all.

Ganpati Bappa Morya!

Republic Day

At Aadarsh Classes, we honor Republic Day with deep respect. The flag is proudly hoisted by a respected army personnel. All students wear a uniform dress code. A special moment is when one girl represents Bharat Mata. The classroom is transformed with lights, flowers, and balloons.

Jai Hind! Jai Bharat!

Independence Day

Independence Day is celebrated with pride. The classroom is adorned with lights, flowers, and balloons. A rally moves through Nampur with a live band playing patriotic songs. Students walk in perfect discipline, carrying the spirit of freedom.

Vande Mataram!

Annual Gathering

A grand event showcasing talents from 5th to 10th standard in dance, drama, and more. Prize distribution by 10th boys, Trust Trophy for long-standing students, and the secret reveal of Student of the Year. Everything from invitation cards to trophies is crafted by students.

Where memories are made, and excellence is honored.

Student of the Year

The most prestigious award recognizing academic brilliance, discipline, and contribution. Based on years at the institute, cultural participation, marks, attendance, and notebook marks. Top 10 shortlisted, audience poll to select Top 5, then a final anonymous paper evaluation. The winner is known only to Sharad Sawant Sir and revealed at the event.

An award that inspires, a moment remembered forever.
